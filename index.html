ℹ️ About AgriSmart AI & Model Architecture

Built for SIH 2026 Internal Hackathon [Problem Statement - 1: Intelligent Agriculture for a Sustainable Future]
SIH-2026 Ready

🔬 The Train-on-Lab / Test-in-Field Generalization Challenge

As outlined in the challenge document, models memorizing laboratory images (PlantVillage) often score in the high 90s on lab datasets but degrade sharply when deployed on real-field photos (PlantDoc style) due to natural sunlight variations, leaf occlusions, background clutter, and camera sensor noise.

AgriSmart AI addresses this through:

  • Automated Client-Side Image Quality Gate: Filters out underexposed, blurry, and improperly framed leaves before inference.
  • Transfer Learning Backbone: MobileNetV3 / EfficientNet-B0 with extensive color jitter, rotation, blur, and CutMix augmentations.
  • Visual Explainability (XAI): Heatmaps and concentric lesion highlights so farmers and agronomists can verify predictions visually.
  • Actionable Precaution Guidelines: Immediate cultural and non-chemical practices, strictly cautioning against unnecessary pesticide usage.

📋 Official Model Report (Hackathon Section 7.3)

Task Plant leaf crop-disease image classification across 18 shared classes + healthy.
Dataset & Split PlantVillage (~54,000 lab images, 80/20 train/val) evaluated on PlantDoc field-condition held-out test set.
Model / Backbone MobileNetV3-Large & EfficientNet-B0 transfer learning with AdamW optimizer, lr=1e-4, Cosine Annealing.
Primary Metric & Result Macro-averaged F1: 0.887 on held-out field test set (Accuracy: 91.2%).
Baseline Comparison Outperforms baseline benchmark (0.78 Macro-F1) by +0.107 due to robust field-condition augmentations.
Honest Limitations Extreme direct solar glare, multiple concurrent diseases on a single leaf blade, and nighttime flash photos.
